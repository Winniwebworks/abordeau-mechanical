About the Company

Full-service HVAC, run the way a trade business should be.

A. Bordeau Mechanical LLC is a New Hampshire-based heating and cooling company built on a simple idea: tell the customer the truth, quote a fair price, and do the work right the first time.

We work on the systems that actually heat and cool New Hampshire homes — oil and gas burners, hydronic baseboard and radiant systems, forced air furnaces, ductless mini splits, central air, on-demand water heaters, and oil tanks. Whether it's a mid-winter breakdown, a full system replacement, or a scheduled tune-up, you get a licensed, owner-operated crew that shows up and explains what's actually going on with your equipment.

No upsells, no vague estimates, no runaround. Just honest diagnostics, fair pricing, and workmanship that holds up through a New Hampshire winter.

About the Owner

Andrew Bordeau

Owner & Lead Technician

Andrew Bordeau founded A. Bordeau Mechanical LLC to bring straightforward, dependable HVAC service to New Hampshire homeowners — the kind of service he'd want for his own family. Every job, from a small repair to a full system install, gets his direct attention.
