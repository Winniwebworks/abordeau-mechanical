About the Company

Local HVAC Service Built Around Quality Work

A. Bordeau Mechanical LLC is a locally owned and operated heating and cooling company based in Andover, New Hampshire.

Why Choose A. Bordeau Mechanical?

What you can expect
  • Locally owned and operated
  • 24/7 service availability
  • Residential and light commercial HVAC
  • Fully insured
  • EPA 608 Universal Certified
  • Service and installation for all major makes and models
  • Professional diagnostics and troubleshooting
  • Quality equipment and materials
  • Straightforward recommendations
  • Professional system commissioning
  • Clean and organized workmanship
  • Heating and cooling solutions designed for New Hampshire conditions

Owner Andrew Bordeau takes a hands-on approach to HVAC service and installation, with a focus on quality workmanship, proper system setup, straightforward communication, and solutions designed around each customer's property and needs.

We provide residential and light commercial HVAC services ranging from troubleshooting and repair to complete heating and cooling system replacements, new installations, ductwork, zoning, hydronic heating, water heating, and system controls.

Whether you're dealing with an unexpected equipment failure, planning an HVAC upgrade, or building a completely new system, our goal is to provide professional service and equipment you can depend on.

Straightforward Service. Quality Workmanship. Local HVAC Expertise.
